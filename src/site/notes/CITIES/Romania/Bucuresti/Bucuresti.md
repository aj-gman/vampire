#bucuresti, #city of joy, or so it was foretold. 
In our times, it is home to the largest concentration of [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]], Bucharest has seen a stable vampiric population kept in line by a regime of full transparency: the Order truly has eyes everywhere and, as such, lets the other covenants operate as individualities, debating in the Council, but the Dragons have the veto, they've always had it, they always will. The shadowy figure at the center of it all, [[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Characters/Ordo Dracul/Generalul\|the Grand Architect of the Bucharestinian Lodge]], is rumored to be Dracula himself, although none live long enough to tell such tall tales.

## Big Picture
Bucharest is old and does not want to change. Every change has been etched in stone before your time. The illusory veil of "it gets better" has been put over the eyes of a few generations with no success. The politicians are corrupt, hand in hand with the rich and dangerous. Supernaturals are just part of this puzzle, contributing in the same ways, extracting value from the system until it'll be dead. It's unsustainable, but few people care.

[[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul#Bucuresti\|The Bucharestinian Lodge of the Ordo Dracul]] sees all and hears all. Its punishments are swift, yet deadly. It rarely, if ever, waits for other covenants to acts.

## The Covenants
### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Invictus\|Invictus]]
*~30 Kindred in 2025.*
[[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Bucuresti Map\|With a Regency in the heart of]] [[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Bucuresti\|Bucuresti]], the [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Invictus\|Invictus]] have a political advantage due to their orientation towards Mortals. Their Primogen, [[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Characters/Invictus/Mihail\|Mihail]], is an old [[Core/Universal Lore/Clans/Nosferatu\|Nosferatu]] whose use of [[Oaths\|Oaths]] has built him quite the favorable pyramid. Even then, the [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Invictus\|Invictus]] are not as oppressive as their [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]] leaders and, with their position merely as providers for **Blood**, they are kept in line by their own meritocratic (and cannibalistic) tendencies. Nevertheless, their influence on Mortals has been spreading in the last 30 years, with their hands in various institutions including the mayorship, the theatres and the nightlife.

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Circle of the Crones\|Circle of the Crones]]
*~10 Kindred in 2025, with more [[Core/Universal Lore/Moroi\|Moroi]] families (~30 people) residing in their territory.*
[[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Bucuresti Map\|With a Regency in the in-betweens of]] [[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Bucuresti\|Bucuresti]], the [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Circle of the Crones\|Crones]] have been isolated by the [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]]. This movement has pushed their Primogen, [[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Characters/Crones/Nina\|Nina]], to distance herself even further from the political institutions of the Night, pursuing self-sustaining isolationism. The [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Circle of the Crones\|Crones]] remain a solace for those seeking refuge from [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul\|persecution]], as they and only they know how to navigate the tunnels, parks and buildings of Crangasi.
