#npc #bucuresti 
What appears to be a woman in her thirties is actually a [[Core/Universal Lore/Clans/Mekhet\|Mekhet]] of quite an age, pre-[[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]], who is said to have visions. 
Although she mostly keeps to herself, [[CITIES/Romania/Bucuresti/Characters/Crones/Nina\|Nina]] is still the Order's declared head of the [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Circle of the Crones\|Circle]], which means she is invited to the Primogen meetings. There, she usually send Neonates, as she cannot be bothered to attend what she regards as a waste of her unlife.
![NPC-Nina.jpg\|300](/img/user/Pictures/NPC-Nina.jpg)
**Appearance:** Sunken Cheeks and Sharp Nails, she is close to a Masquerade breach. White-blue hair that seems ageless, usually dressed in flowy clothings and reeking of blood. Hates children.
