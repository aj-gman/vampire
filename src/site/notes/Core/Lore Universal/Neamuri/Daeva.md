## _The Ones You Don't Want To Say No To..._
_She's the one that got away. He's the guy with long hair and piercings you've seen across the room. She's smiling at you like she wants you to put her in a locker room and just... He's a demon just out for blood. The perfume and cologne hides away all the smell of rotting flesh they inevitably have. Even if you know what they are, you do not care. Their sweet body tastes so divine... And you are just exploited. They're the toxic relationship you cannot get away from. The cult you inevitably found yourself after the divorce. The parent you never want to disappoint because they love you so._

That's what the average mortal thinks about us. We move quick and we look damn good doing it. We can rock anything - death metal rockstar, flashy corporate, old money passenger princess. Even "homeless urban" looks good on us.

We control the keystone of human misery: wanting, desire. Yet, as simple as we feed and caress, we can never truly feel love, and that is our course: we get addicted, we do not fall in love. Passion fuels only the hunt, the next orgasm and the next cocaine line.

**Have you ever truly felt happiness? 'Cause I haven't.**

### Blestemul Neamului
**The Wanton Curse**: You can taste the romance in everything - art, culture, fine wine (that you have to vomit out later), but most of all the sweet, orgasmic feeling of blood in your mouth. Mortals are not just food, they are your obsession, growing with every sip. **Drink more than once from the same vessel and you risk becoming emotionally dependent on your prey - mortal, Kindred or otherwise.**

### The Beast / Fiara
The Daeva Beast is a beautiful angel, and it comes and takes control, leaving the Man to watch as mute dissociated witness. Their sinew grace leaves mortals breathless and willing to offer their own throats to the predator among them. The experience of the Daeva Kiss is orgasmic, a feeling of all-encompassing pleasure that grips them in silent adoration.
