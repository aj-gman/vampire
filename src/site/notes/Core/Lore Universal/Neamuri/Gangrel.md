## _The Ones You Can't Kill..._
_The dogs barking in somebody's backyard that you cross the street to not have to deal with. The ravens crowing at your windows for breadcrumbs and death. The drunk truck driver that you refused and groped you on the way out. The beast walking on all fours. When they're around, you're no longer top of the food chain. Inevitable death comes for us all - but this one's got a taste for your blood and bones._

_They call themselves primal, you call them feral. You're dead before you can even think of anything. Their teeth are as sharp as the spikes of Dracula. Laughing at your futile attempts to shoot them - and God knows, you've tried everything: machineguns, flamethrowers, and they just grow stronger._

_They're the CEO that would do anything to keep his company profitable. The king of the jungle - urban or otherwise. The reason why man discovered fire, Diogenes sitting in a barrel and pissing on Plato's philosophies. Savagery like that comes only when you embrace the Beast._

Those who think like that are weak. They see the Beast as an enemy, something to be ashamed of, something to chain. No, as one of us, you can't stop and you don't even want to. You are a creature of the night, just like the wolves, just like the bears. They fear us for what we are - the truth.

The night has a thousand mouths. You're never alone with us. But it comes with a price - there is no me, there is no you. The beast is all-encompassing: it turns your nails into claws, fermenting under your skin, itching to come out and play.

**Come out and play, you sick fuck.**

### Clan Bane / Blestemul de Neam
**The Feral Curse**: The Beast breaks through their skin to protect them from the bad, bad world. It's harder to keep track of who you were before you were what you are now, it's harder still to keep track of who is who - are you the Beast, or the other way around? **Frenzy Checks and any other attempts to resist the Beast come at a penalty equal to your Blood Potency.**

### The Beast / Fiara
The monster inside does not speak in tongues, it does not indulge in itself. It merely shows its master the wisest choice. It prompts the Kindred to think with self-conservation and survival instincts, not with rationality or emotions. What good is a Beast if it's dead? It's voice is raspy, it's teeth are sharp. Whenever it devours, you find it difficult to close up wounds. It's Kiss feels good in the same way hard liquor or a cigar would feel good.
