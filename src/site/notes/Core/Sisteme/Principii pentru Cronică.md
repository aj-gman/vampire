For any good Vampire[^1] game you need a good cityscape to build upon, a city whose residents have etched history into its every nook and cranny. 

For that, I've constructed the basic vampiric structure for [[Romania/Bucuresti/Altele/București\|București]] (Our Times), [[Romania/Bucuresti/Personaje/Coterii/The Countess\|Bucuresti (Interwar)]], [[Rome\|Rome]] and [[Louisiana\|Louisiana]], each with distinct pasts and presents.

The Chronicle is what the players go through and explore, their perception of the backstory and their movement of the needle forward. [[Templates/Structure of a Chronicle\|Here's how to tell it.]]

##### make it PERSONAL
- It's my enemies, it's my life, it's my choices. Yes, the systems have been built already, but as a creature of the night I can tear down mortals with a swing. Yet, I don't. Why?
- It always comes back to the same old adage. People don't change, societies don't change. What's the point?
- Vampires don't have to sniff, don't have to breathe, don't have to try. Yet, they do.
- Your players are both AUDIENCE and PROTAGONISTS.

##### make it GOTHIC
- The city is old, falling apart, decrepit. Everyone's hiding everywhere. There's darkness and neon signs, but it's all always a facade. Nobody cuts to the chase, unless forced to.
- No Kindred cares for other Covenants, they disregard antagonistic beliefs. As voyagers between the Covenants, you're forced to defend yourself through speech and action.

##### make it HORROR.
- Blood is always coursing through mortal veins. You can feel its every pump, smell its every beat. It only takes a moment.
- We drink blood. We leave corpses. We're used to it. We don't mind the rampant murder, the scared mortals we leave behind. It's our nature, after all.
- The NIGHT makes monsters out of all of us. Humanity is a struggle for all, dead or dying. Keeping your sanity while plotting is nearly impossible.

##### make it REQUIEM.
- Even if you reveal your insidious nature, people don't care or don't believe their own eyes. You break them, but nobody's out hunting for you.
- Vampires are no longer hiding in the shadows, they are inhumane and brutal and horribly disfigured.


[^1]: Masquerade, Requiem, Undying and beyond
