Animalism is a #discipline shared by the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Gangrel\|Gangrel]] and the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Ventrue\|Ventrue]].

As the name suggests, this Discipline allows the user to more closely reconnect with his own Beast, allowing them to communicate with animals and even command them with dominating authority. As the power of the Kindred grows, he may even attempt to command the Beast inside humans or even other Kindred.

Animalism does by no means allow one to rekindle with nature in a druid-like way, in fact, it perpetuates a strict hierarchy based on raw power: animals affected by this Discipline can be commanded to perform certain tasks for the Kindred. The stronger one's personality (either Presence or Manipulation), the more influence they gain over animals.
