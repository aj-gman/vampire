Auspex is the Signature #discipline of the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Mekhet\|Mekhet]]. Using this Discipline allows the Kindred to have supernatural sensory abilities, like reading another's thoughts or just perceiving smells, tastes, noises with heightened senses.

This becomes especially useful in social situations, where one can more easily manipulate feelings, as well as gain knowledge without an actual need to understand how people think.
