Celerity is a physical #discipline  shared by the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Daeva\|Daeva]] and the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Mekhet\|Mekhet]]. The Discipline gives the Kindred unnatural speed, being able to move and interact with objects much quicker than any other.

- Passive: All the dots are added to the Kindred's Defense (VTR) and to her Dodge roll if she is actively defending.
- Active: For each Vitae spent, the Kindred may:
    - Immediately move to the head of the Initiative Queue for one turn.
    - Give her inhuman speed, granting her the ability to cross rivers and even run up walls.
    - Interrupt another character (before they roll, but after they declare their intentions) by taking an Instant Action (to a maximum of 1 Extra Instant Action, thus no more than 2 Attacks per round per Celerity user).

> [[Core/Sisteme/Reguli de casă\|Reguli de casă]]: As an Active use, the Kindred can apply her defense to all firearms attack for a turn by spending 1 Vitae.
