a.k.a. **Înrobirea**

Dominate is the Signature #discipline of the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Ventrue\|Ventrue]]. It gives the Kindred unnatural willpower over others, being able to commandeer their thoughts, manipulate their minds and force them to do things otherwise unspeakable. 

Eye contact is a myth perpetuated by mortal culture, but it has some truth in the way the Discipline is used, the Dominator needing to look deep into the target's eyes in order to look directly at their soul in order to be able to influence it. However, Kindred are not thwarted by gouged eyes or sunglasses, as they can find another way to stare into one's soul.
