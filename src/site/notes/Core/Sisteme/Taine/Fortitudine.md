#discipline 
Fortitude is a physical #discipline shared by the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Gangrel\|Gangrel]] and the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Ventrue\|Ventrue]]. The Discipline gives the Kindred supernatural resistance to damage of all kinds, transforming them into an immovable object to be reckoned with.

_Also referred to as Resilience in Vampire: The Requiem._

- **Passive:** Each dot that is invested into Fortitude adds an extra point to their Stamina, thus increasing their Health.
- **Active:** For 1 Vitae, add your Fortitude points as temporary Armor, reducing all incoming damage for one turn.
