#discipline 
Majesty is the signature #discipline of the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Daeva\|Daeva]] clan. The Discipline allow the Kindred to charm mortals and supernatural being alike, convincing them with ease to be by their side.

Majesty differs from [[Core/Sisteme/Taine/Dominare\|Dominare]] as it is a willing admiration of the Kindred rather than a forceful dominion, which comes with the (dis)advantage that the mortal retains their free will, allowing them to be creative and passionate, but also being able to question their own loyalty.

Anyone (mortal or otherwise) can attempt to resist Majesty for a scene by spending 1 Willpower point and rolling **Composure + Resolve** against the Kindred's **Presence + Persuasion**. However, even on a success, the target must continue to spend Willpower points while they are in the presence of the Kindred to avoid being charmed by them.

>[[Core/Sisteme/Reguli de casă\|Reguli de casă]]: Confidant (Level 2) has been removed, shifting Green Eyes (Level 3) & Entrancement (Level 4) exactly one level down, to Level 2 and Level 3 respectively. Idol has been kept at Level 5, which has given way to a skill from V20, Summon (Level 4)

Majesty is not a replacement for good social roleplay, in fact, it serves to enhance it. No VTR Discipline is a winning ticket: taking finesse and precision to pull it off, a smart Kindred hones his relationships over long periods of time.
