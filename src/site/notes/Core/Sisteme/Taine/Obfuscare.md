#discipline 
Obfuscate is a #discipline shared by the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Mekhet\|Mekhet]] and [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Nosferatu\|Nosferatu]] clans. The Discipline gives the Kindred the ability to become invisible and silent, thus granting them the ability to vanish and disappear at whim.

Theoretically, as the name implies, the Kindred doesn't _actually_ turn invisible, rather they conceal themselves in such a way to fool those that are watching, quite literally hiding in plain sight. This implies a sort of hypnosis effect similar to that of [[Core/Sisteme/Taine/Maiestate\|Maiestate]] or [[Core/Sisteme/Taine/Dominare\|Dominare]], only it is used to hide the user from the prying eyes of onlookers.
