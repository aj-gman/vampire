Nightmare is the Signature #discipline  of the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Nosferatu\|Nosferatu]] clan, a unique twist not found in Masquerade. The closest thing to Nightmare in VTM would be Dementation, the ability of the Malkavian clan. The Discipline allows the Kindred to bring out the worst in others, play with their fears and manipulate their sensations to cause abject terror.
