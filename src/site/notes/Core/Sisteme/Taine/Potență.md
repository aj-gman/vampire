Potence is a physical #discipline shared by the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Daeva\|Daeva]] and the [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Nosferatu\|Nosferatu]]. The Discipline allow the Kindred to perform supernatural feats of strength, shattering bone as if it was butter and lifting impossible amounts of weight. 
*It is referred to as **Vigor** in Vampire The Requiem.*

- **Passive:** Each dot that is invested into Potence allows the vampire to add an extra die to all Strength rolls at no cost.
    > [[Core/Sisteme/Reguli de casă\|Reguli de casă]]: **Active:** For 1 Vitae, turn as many points of damage dealt as dots in Potence into Aggravated Damage instead of Superficial Damage.
