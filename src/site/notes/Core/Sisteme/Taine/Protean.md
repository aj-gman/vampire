Protean is the Signature #discipline of the Gangrel clan. The Discipline gives the Kindred unnatural abilities of shapeshifting and altering their own form, mending with animals or even the earth itself.

All of the Discipline's Abilities can store Traits/Animals etc. up to the current number of dots the Kindred has in Protean. In order to change them, the Kindred must spend 1 Vitae while being in an Unmarked Grave or in a Primeval Miasma. The changes take 1 Night to manifest.

> [[Core/Sisteme/Reguli de casă\|Reguli de casă]]: In order to encourage players to spend their starting points however they want, Predatory Aspect and Unmarked Grave have been switched, with Predatory Aspect now being Level 1 and Unmarked Grave being Level 2 respectively.
