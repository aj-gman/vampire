#quasi-discipline 
[[blood-sorcery-rites-of-damnation?filters=0_0_0_0_0_100001).

A type of [[Magick\|Magick]] specific to the [[Core/Lore Universal/Jurăminte/Zgripțuroaicele\|Zgripțuroaicele]].
Crúac caps Humanity at (10 - Crúac rating). Sacrifices require 1 Vitae and a meaningful object. Requests can be addressed to gods of nature, to the Vitae itself, to the Beast or to any other facet of the Mother.

##### Themes
- **Linked Themes:** [[Magick#Creation\|Creation]] & [[Magick#**Protection**\|Protection]]
- **Motif:** Corrupting, Wild, Pagan

##### Dice Pool
- Manipulation + Primary Theme for improvised or Mastered Crúac Rites,
- Manipulation + Occult + Crúac for Learned Crúac Rites.
