=== Starter Questions ===

* Does your character think of themselves as good or evil?
* Do you think your character is good or evil?
* Is your character easily willing to kill mortals, hesitant, or do they avoid it at all costs?
* • Is your character willing to kill other vampires?
* • How much does your character enjoy their unlife?
* • Does your character still have the capacity to love?
* • How loyal is your character to their covenant?
* • How loyal is your character to their coterie?
* • Does your character believe in the Prince’s rule or think they should overthrow the government?
* • How close to their Beast is your character?
* • Does your character miss their mortality?

=== Climbing the Ladder ===

* Confront Vulnerability - A moment when you were most vulnerable or had most to lose (hubris). An ideal moment to learn or become fascinated with the supernatural. For a Coterie moment, why did you join the group?
* Believe Someone Has Answers - Dizzy with questions, you stumble upon somebody who had the answers. Who took initiative in the group, even if they were proved wrong afterwards?
* Meet Your Maker/Find Power - What happened after you were subjected to something you could no longer deny? How did you deal with the Embrace and with your sire? 
** Who in the Coterie did you look up to, the one true Strength.
* Find Fear/Darkness - There is something dark inside you, something that even the Beast growls at. How do you deal with whatever's been broken inside you? Perhaps its your Nightmare - the Kindred you see and you fear you'll become or, perhaps, how you keep hidden the fact that you're capable of far worse. 
** In your Coterie, what is the bad habbit nobody can seem to shake?
* Leave a Witness - a Choice or a Mistake? why did you tell a complete stranger that vampires exist - or, worse, why do you have a secret lover, a hunter or somebody else that you left slip your entire personality. What did you reveal and how do you deal with that? Breach.
** Someone Else Knows. Your coterie committed something quite heinous. A buried body in the forest that you've never spoken about afterwards. And, worse, who knows enough to keep you in check?
* Suffer Your Defects - You saw the curse for what it really was and experienced its limitations. How you almost died because of your new condition or, worse, how your condition turned somebody else into a ghoul of what they were before.
** When did Ben - or whoever was the other core member, died?
* Beg Someone, Anyone to Fix You - And then you ran to other monsters, begging them to take you in, to help you understand your condition. Perhaps, then, you found a mentor - who might have left you with more questions and favors owed than truths.
* Remember Your Victims - You've killed, it's impossible to avoid it forever. What was your first kill, accidental or otherwise, and how did it teach you more about you? Blame yourself for the death or, worse, write the name of the body on your sheet and draw lines to two other individuals. Their friends.
** Someone your coterie used, abused & discarded like a piece of gum. How did you kill them - or worse, what has transpired in their absence?
* Embrace a Wrong - when's the time you broke something from your code, from your virtues and it felt good, although so wrong? Acceptance at its worst - lying through your teeth, betraying and revelling in that danger and in that disgust. Or, worse, you've got a stalker who believes you've wronged him terribly.
* Find Like Minds/Allies - What sort of vampire did you decide to be? What covenant did you join, if any, and why? Perhaps they wouldn't let you in - so, how did you show them you were willing, if  not desperate, to be part of their group? Or, worse, now that you've gotten accepted and you're getting comfortable in your role, there's others under you that want your seat and that will stop at nothing to get it.
** While these people are not your family, they're someone you'd trust to support you in dire times. They may be members of a closely-held covenant or another coterie you've made a pact with.
* Carpe Noctem - Power, Control or Authority. At some point, you will get bored of self-improvement and you'll want something more. Perhaps its shaping yourself to be perfect, perhaps its forbidden knowledge you seek. You betrayed another Kindred to get higher up or, perhaps, you've enabled the same vicious cycle because "you'll do it right".
** What does the coterie want, above all? Are you taking over an institution, a covenant? What is the change you want to see in the world?
* Winding Up - now tell the story as it is. Try to streamline your work and get it together.
