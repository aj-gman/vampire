The result of [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]] research and experimenting, this [Quasi-Discipline](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Disciplines#Quasi-Disciplines "Category:Disciplines") allows those Kindred to circumvent their ontological state of being, developing resistances to things thought impossible. Each Coil allows the scholar to develop so-called Scales, which are specific rituals that, while torturous and risky, can grant non-scholars almost the same capabilities. Coils are determined by Vices, which the Dragons seek to control and overpower.

**Coils are a specialized, more scientific form of [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Magick\|Magick]].** Coils last longer, are harder to obtain and are less inclusive, being focused on overcoming the vampiric condition's drawbacks.

**However, there are two distinctions that make Coils special:** firstly, Coils don't follow the usual Blood Sorcery themes, instead they follow the Seven Deadly Vices. Secondly, the duration is determined by the level of the Ritual (Hours > Nights > Permanent) and the sum of Vitae spent and Blood Potency.

- An Improvised Ritual of the Coil from a Kindred with 3 BP and 2 Vitae spent will last 5 hours.
- A Learned Ritual of the Coil from a Kindred with 1 BP and 1 Vitae spent will last 2 Nights.
- A Mastered Ritual of the Coil from a Kindred with 2 BP and 1 Vitae spent will last 3 Months.

#### Thematic Vices
- **Envy,** which drenches any Blood Bonds that the scholar makes in thickly-sweet Vitae, granting him increased resistance to the Vinculum, but also enhancing his blood to the point of addiction for those who taste it.
- **Gluttony,** which allows the scholar to quench their thirst for Vitae, being able to sustain themselves with the nourishment of their own soul.
- **Greed,** which allows the scholar to amass knowledge and power incomprehensible otherwise, granting them increased bonuses to skill rolls.
- **Lust,** which permits the scholar to cheat his deadly nature, becoming mortal once more, consuming human food and even having a beating heart.
- **Pride,** which grants the adept immense volition to resist his natural fears generated by the Curse, being able to walk in the sun and touch fire without the Beast entering frenzy.
- **Sloth,** which helps the scholar endure age-long torpor without losing himself to time.
- **Wrath,** which forces the Beast into submission, controlling it as if it were a child in need of care.
