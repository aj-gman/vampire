Blood Sorcery is an umbrella term for a variety of rituals, tricks and incantations that fall under the category of #quasi-discipline, not being as overbearingly powerful as #discipline but not accessible by mere mortals without proper knowledge. Both [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Lancea et Sanctum\|Lancea et Sanctum]] and [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Zgripturoaicele\|Zgripturoaicele]] practice Blood Sorcery, even if their specialties vary quite a bit in terms of themes, methods and results.

## Summary
Rituals require **Sacrifice** in order to conjure a **Request** and hopefully receive a **Response** without **Risk**. The request requires a roll, that varies depending on the type of Blood Sorcery[^2] and experience of the caster[^3]. **Risk** is determined by the ritual's advanced **Factors** (Potency, Duration, Range, Scale & Size[^4].
Whenever casting, **rituals take time** depending on the caster's experience with them: I**mprovised (1 hour), Learned (10 minutes), Mastered (4 turns)**. The speed of the ritual can be improved by rushing it, up to a (-5) penalty to the casting dice.

## Outcome
After the Ritual is complete (even if the caster receives a positive Response, even if they don't), the Storyteller must weigh the Risk and see whether or not there is any supernatural danger approaching the ritualist.
**Weighing Risk** is done after the Ritual is resolved. The Storyteller rolls whatever the current Risk dice pool is (+2 dice per advanced ritual factor, but reduced by Discipline rating being higher than the Ritual's rating, it being Learned, Altars, Additional Vitae spent specifically for this purpose etc.). On a failure, the Risk is weighed in the ritualist's favor, meaning nothing special occurs. On a success or above, the Storyteller may inflict a **Backlash**[^5].

## Making Up Rites
### Steps
**The default rite has moderate power, lasts only one turn, requires touch, affects a single target and cannot be larger than an average human.** 
- Increasing any of these Factors raises the difficulty of the dice roll by +1 per Factor per "severity level".[^6] 
- However, before raising the difficulty of the roll, the player may spend "free" Factors equal to their dots in the ritual's Theme;.

### Themes
There are 6 themes, each corresponding with a different type of applicable sorcery. Two of these are more easily accessible to [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Vrajitorie\|Vrajitorie]] users, namely **Creation** & **Protection**. Two others are more accessible to [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Taumaturgie\|Theban Ritualists]], namely **Divination** & **Transmutation**. The last two are equally accessible to all ritualists irrespective of covenant.

##### Creation
- Conjure items of variable sizes and mind. At high levels, be able to create living creatures that might just be able to think for themselves. It's much easier to create predators, insects, plagues than medicines or nourishment. Ideas: Creatures, Objects, Sensory Phenomena, Supernatural Phenomena.
##### Destruction
- Deal damage to a target or raise the sharpness of a sword. Erode the defense of others. Destroy one's mind or the building they live in. Shatter their dreams. It is pure rot, not psychological. Ideas: Degradation, Direct Damage, Upgraded Damage.
##### Divination
- Seek truths in omens and voices of the night. Go beyond. Manifest destiny. Look into the past or through the possible futures. Ideas: Revelations, Perception, Triggers.
##### **Guidance**
- Change how the world at large reacts to a certain event or person. There is no alteration of fate, just a subtle leash imposed on another, perhaps a charm of good luck or a manipulation of the dice. Ideas: Blessings, Curses, Mental Shifts, Supernatural Changes.
##### **Protection**
- Defend someone from physical harm, alleviate one's Banes, even heal or remove [Conditions](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditions "Conditions"). Ideas: Healing, Mundane Dangers, Supernatural Threats, Banes.
##### **Transmutation**
- Alter the physical makeup or supernatural nature of any given subject. You cannot alter the Vitae, but using it in Transmutation becomes easy: exsanguinate from a distance, reanimate flesh, remove one's control over their body. Ideas: Creatures, Objects & Substances.
