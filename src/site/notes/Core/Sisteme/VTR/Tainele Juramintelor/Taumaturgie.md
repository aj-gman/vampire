#quasi-discipline 
[[blood-sorcery-rites-of-damnation?filters=0_0_0_0_0_100001).

A type of [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Magick\|Magick]] closely guarded by the [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Lancea et Sanctum\|Lancea et Sanctum]].
Theban Sorcery requires Humanity at a level equal to or higher than the Miracle attempted by the sorcerer. Sacrifices require 1 Vitae, 1 Willpower and a Sacrament. Requests must be addressed to a specific saint, God or other religious figure that the caster truly believes in.

##### Themes
- **Linked Themes:** [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Magick#Divination\|Divination]] + [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Magick#**Transmutation**\|Transmutation]]
- **Motif:** Judgmental, Holy, Deliberate

##### Dice Pool
- Intelligence + Primary Theme for improvised or Mastered Theban Miracles,
- Intelligence + Academics + Theban Sorcery for Learned Theban Miracles.
