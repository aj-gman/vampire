## _The Ones You Can't Kill..._
_The dogs barking in somebody's backyard that you cross the street to not have to deal with. The ravens crowing at your windows for breadcrumbs and death. The drunk truck driver that you refused and groped you on the way out. The beast walking on all fours. When they're around, you're no longer top of the food chain. Inevitable death comes for us all - but this one's got a taste for your blood and bones._

_They call themselves primal, you call them feral. You're dead before you can even think of anything. Their teeth are as sharp as the spikes of Dracula. Laughing at your futile attempts to shoot them - and God knows, you've tried everything: machineguns, flamethrowers, and they just grow stronger._

_They're the CEO that would do anything to keep his company profitable. The king of the jungle - urban or otherwise. The reason why man discovered fire, Diogenes sitting in a barrel and pissing on Plato's philosophies. Savagery like that comes only when you embrace the Beast._

Those who think like that are weak. They see the Beast as an enemy, something to be ashamed of, something to chain. No, as one of us, you can't stop and you don't even want to. You are a creature of the night, just like the wolves, just like the bears. They fear us for what we are - the truth.

The night has a thousand mouths. You're never alone with us. But it comes with a price - there is no me, there is no you. The beast is all-encompassing: it turns your nails into claws, fermenting under your skin, itching to come out and play.

**Come out and play, you sick fuck.**

### Clan Bane / Blestemul de Neam
**The Feral Curse**: The Beast breaks through their skin to protect them from the bad, bad world. It's harder to keep track of who you were before you were what you are now, it's harder still to keep track of who is who - are you the Beast, or the other way around? **Frenzy Checks and any other attempts to resist the Beast come at a penalty equal to your Blood Potency.**

### The Beast / Fiara
The monster inside does not speak in tongues, it does not indulge in itself. It merely shows its master the wisest choice. It prompts the Kindred to think with self-conservation and survival instincts, not with rationality or emotions. What good is a Beast if it's dead? It's voice is raspy, it's teeth are sharp. Whenever it devours, you find it difficult to close up wounds. It's Kiss feels good in the same way hard liquor or a cigar would feel good.

## Within the Covenants

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Carthian Movement\|Carthian Movement]]
Change is a savage enterprise. The dead don’t like new ideas, but the Gangrel are dynamic in a vicious sort of way. They are the roar of the revolution and the messengers of the movement, traveling city to city. Some began as souls too wild to domesticate. Others are former hounds of the First Estate, tired of their masters’ abuse. Their nature confounds the tactics of the establishment. How do you threaten the havens of those who sleep in the earth? How do you bring the heat down on those who can skip town at a moment’s notice?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Circle of the Crones\|Circle of the Crones]]
Whole murders of Gangrel perch in the Mother’s Army. The clan’s oral tradition teems with fierce mother figures like Ekhidna. Young Savages are easy recruits. Primal blood sings in tune with primordial magic. Hierophants lead Wild Hunts in the form of howling wolves. Feral witches roar to the stars and tap the wisdom of the dark earth. Who better to explore what it is to be a monster, than those so intimate with the Beast?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Invictus\|Invictus]]
For all of their cagey instincts, road lust, and hunger for freedom, some Gangrel respond well to strong alphas. And who is more alpha than the First Estate? These Gangrel find their niches as the knights and growling hounds of lords and ladies. But some rise higher. They learn to translate their predatory natures to politics. They attain savage nobility. For so too does the Invictus respond well to alphas, and who is more elementally alpha than the get of lion gods and demon wolves?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Lancea et Sanctum\|Lancea et Sanctum]]
Some Gangrel get religious when they realize they can call all of God’s beasts to his vengeful purpose. The Savages make useful converts to the Chapel and the Spear. Who better to travel the world’s hidden places in the search of relics? Faith molds people, and monsters are made of even more malleable clay. Some Gangrel become bestial seraphs, the wings of bats and owls ripping from out their backs. Some angels have more cause than others to say, “Be not afraid.”

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]]
The Gangrel fascinate the Dragons. More often than not, it is the Order that approaches the Gangrel. Their protean bodies merit study. Endless variation that breaks the monotony of the eternally dead flesh. The Savages have more to offer the Dragons than just the physical. Creatures willing to leave behind their physical identities attain the sort of mindset needed to chase the elusive mysteries of the Ordo Dracul. Who better to evolve the Curse? Who else has that elemental audacity?
