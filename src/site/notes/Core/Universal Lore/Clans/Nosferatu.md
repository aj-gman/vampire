## _The Ones You Fear..._
*The stray dog that keeps following you asking for food that you're scared is just going to take a pound of flesh. The reason you keep turning your head when you're going home at night. Why horror stories online seem so scary - anonymity, death, despair. Even when you try to make friends with them, something's off.*

*Like there's a serial killer hiding in that brain of theirs (if they still have one and it isn't rotting), waiting to pounce. Either it's a foul smell, a mental disorder or something much more visible, like a deformation on their face. But it's a clear message: they are broken, no matter how much they try to fit in. It's pointless.*

*So they haunt everything and everyone, like ghosts of Christmas Past, reveling in the terror they generate. They hang around on the street, in the sewers, hiding away from prying eyes, they may kill, kidnap and even eat people. But it's all owed to that sad look in their eyes, isn't it?*

*How can I be sad when I look at the biggest, meanest motherfucker in the eyes and he walks away like a big baby? Nobody wants to talk to me, nobody wants to be next to me, nobody wants to get infested with whatever I've got. But that's alright. I'll make sure they do.*

*There is nothing you can do when fear crawls at your door. Paranoia. Dread. Disgust. These are emotions deeply hidden behind your human mask. Kindred or Kin, you all fall down and kneel to horror willingly - flags will burn, countries will fall, swords will rust, dominators will be killed and icons will get old.*

**Fear, however, was with humans even earlier than fire.**

### Clan Bane / Blestemul de Neam
**The Lonely Curse**: You are an avatar of horror, the antagonist in any mortal story. Dread and discomfort oozes from you, scabbing everything over in the putrid film of your rotting soul’s exhaust. Your body is warped, or the world around you warps. This could manifest in ways grotesque or subtle. Fear and all its gibbering siblings come easy for you. Most other forms of social communion do not. Yours is a lonely Requiem. **A trait, physical, mental or social, causes you extreme anguish due to its horrible deformation. You are cursed to be creepy to all, even your best of friends.**

### The Beast / Fiara
The Haunts' Beast is the rebellion of the flesh and mind in jerking stop-motion, a hissing horror show that drives them. The Kiss is pure, unadulterated fear, that shuts down the mind and floods it with adrenaline. The Strigoy experiences the foreign vitae burrowing into his body like worms gnawing on his dead flesh.

## Within the Covenants

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Carthian Movement\|Carthian Movement]]
Sometimes, the politics of change are the politics of fear. The people should not be afraid of their government. The government should be afraid of its dead. Underground networks and subterranean lairs are handy things when taking on the Man. Who better to wage guerrilla war on the entrenched establishment? Who better to initiate a reign of terror?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Circle of the Crones\|Circle of the Crones]]
Some Haunts look at their own visages and see a reflection of the Crone. Divinity is terrible for mortals to look upon. These hags and hierophants preach that they are blessed with a divine beauty recognized by eyes ancient and deep that operate under different aesthetics. The Nosferatu murmur about the Gods Below. Why not worship the Hidden Ones more directly? Who better to become a perfect monster than one unfettered by a human face?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Invictus\|Invictus]]
The First Estate knows the value of Haunt agents. Some Nosferatu creep to great heights. There are cities where rat lords and worm ladies hold nightmare court below. The Invictus of other clans are forced to descend, to speak courtesies on gritted teeth to the Haunt lackeys who guide them down, hoping the wretches do not abandon them in the dark. What was that Machiavelli said about fear?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Lancea et Sanctum\|Lancea et Sanctum]]
Some Haunts look at their visages and see the faces of demons. Religion comes easy after that. In centuries past, Nosferatu serving the Spear carried sickness and pestilence as the wrath of Heaven upon humanity. Terrorizing sinners comes so easy to them. This gives their horrible curse a purpose. What wretch would not want to learn that her malady serves a divine plan? Who better to instill the fear of God? Who more fully embodies a divine plague on mankind?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Covenants/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]]
Seclusion, and something to contemplate to fill that seclusion, suits the Nosferatu. The Haunts have strong stomachs and adjust quickly to performing grotesque surgeries and disturbing procedures. Underground sanctums make for ideal laboratories, and Dragons know the value of recruiting local Haunts. Some Nosferatu toil their nights away, subjecting themselves to all manner of esoteric experiments, trying to fix their twisted forms, or transcend them and enter the next stage of change. Who would be more driven to subvert his Curse?
