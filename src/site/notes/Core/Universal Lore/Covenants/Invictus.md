#covenant of successful entrepreneurs that are always on the bleeding edge of the rise. Schemers, spies, politicians, businessmen, everything that gives them more power, they've got a plan for it. Modern regalia, they're perhaps the most inclusive of the Covenants. Anyone who can take the power should do so - every night, from sunset to sunrise. It would be a shame to try to put a [[Core/Universal Lore/Clans/Gangrel\|Gangrel]] in a suit when he belongs on the street, cutting shady deals. To tell you about the Invictus, let me tell you about **your other options**, young childer.

First, our "closest allies", the insane zealots that love swords and chainmail or shotguns and Kevlar vests, depending on their age. It's a bell curve of violence: Elders love shotguns, Ancillae love their zweihanders and Neonates are back to bullets in barrels. Give any of them a target, tell them Jesus or Longinus or another "-us" saint wants them to do mass murder in the name of the Kingdom of God, and they'll burn everything to the ground.

Then, there's the almighty Carthians upholding their equality at any cost. They're trying so hard to be us that they've mimicked our hierarchy while preaching their "everybody should be the same" doctrine, but all they've managed is bureaucracy that falls like a Jenga tower. They have no structure, because they want equality but they don't see themselves as equal amongst themselves and towards others. Idiots.

Or you could live the rest of your unlife as a hobo, asking for money on the street and letting your Disciplines go to waste with the Crones. Go have an orgy in the woods, get three bloodborne diseases and maybe, just maybe, learn some of that blood sorcery before you die from the plague.

Lastly, dear childer, are the hellspawns of medieval torturers that want to cling to myth just as much as they want scientific rigour. A bunch of hypocrites that think they're smarter than everybody just because they observe things instead of actually interacting with anything. I've never seen an Ordo Dracul that's happy, not even for a moment of ecstasy or orgasm. They time their orgasms so they're scientifically efficient. Mad scientists.

You're free to choose, childer. There are many options, but only one is correct. I've raised you here, but I will not hold you down. Perhaps my ways are old-fashioned, childer. I'll let you choose. But remember, I Embraced you, I raised you and I love you. Don't betray me.

#### Fun Facts
- The Invictus believe they are the rightful descendants of the [Camarilla](https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Camarilla_\(VTR\)) and, as such, also refer to themselves as the **First Estate** (with the [Lancea](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Lancea_et_Sanctum "Lancea et Sanctum"), their sometimes close allies, being recognized as the Second Estate).
- Formally, the institution is structured by one's advancement into vampiric society. Those recently Embraced are kept under their sire's thumb, trained for the "release", an honorary event in which the Neonate swears fealty to the Prince. As such, the Invictus has cultivated a [culture of titles](https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Invictus#Positions) that symbolize one's role and power within their organization, while enforcing a form of **polite society** generated by the local conditions[[2\|2]](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Invictus#cite_note-2).
    - This has led to the development of a deep judicatory etiquette generated by internal mindsets and external perceptions. The Invictus love to bathe themselves in class, in Kevlar-lined suits with golden cuffs and expensive cars. They've modelled their success by the desires of their eldest, while appealing to the kingly status that is attributed to them by the [Carthians](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Carthian_Movement "Carthian Movement") with a modicum of poison.
- **Survival of the fittest is the informal law amongst the First Estate.** The only way to occupy a position is to create it yourself or replace the one in charge - sometimes, this means cutting throats, other times it just requires blackmail. Rarely, positions are hereditary, handed over by sire to childer.
