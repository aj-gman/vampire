**RO: Mișcarea, Cartienii**
**Covenant of vampires gathered together under one objective: make unlife better. The first step is agreed upon by all Carthians: equality. What comes next is a matter of vision and personal differences - Carthians may argue amongst themselves, but they always project a crisp, solid image outwards. The more, the merrier: they genuinely believe they'll figure out what political project they put forth once they're already in office. First, however, the Prince must step down or be overthrown.**

- **Advantage:** [Carthian Law](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Carthian_Law "Carthian Law")
- **Vibe:** Modern, Evergreen, Ever-changing, Passionate, Fluent, Smart, Chaotic
- **Stereotypes:** Wealthy Socialite, Trade Unionizer, Student Protester, Carnival Worker, Private Investigator, The Rockstar, Political Artist, Visionary Philosopher, Martyr Activist
- **Album Theme:** [viagr aboys, by Viagra Boys](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nOMpcLIyWZ1lVFspzVeivHsFvuQzQFSYA)

*As much as different voices will tell you, Carthians are not anarchists. They are not the molotov-wielding rabble out in the street protesting against something and standing for nothing. They're in the boardrooms vying for their option, making their voice heard, making sure the Revolution has a seat at the table, whatever that table might be. The Movement wants to be everywhere, all at once. The only issue is, equality of thinking as a goal means everyone has different ways of seeing and enforcing it. There's more Carthian lawyers than Carthian terrorists. Think of that one.*

*At the end of the day, [Carthian Law](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Carthian_Law "Carthian Law") is totalitarian in the way its enforced, its one of the most ruthless top-down systems, because it defies normal rules. The Law works because some higher thing lets it. We can't see that thing, whatever it is, an Elder, Julius Senex himself or even some deity high up on a cloud or in the cosmos. It's got a good heart, because its for the people, for the common good, for the betterment of society. But, the question arises: is a dictatorship of the people even a democracy at all?*

#### Fun Facts
- There is no one official language of the Revolution, but English is spoken by almost every Carthian on the globe.
- Carthian structure varies from city to city, even from borough to borough depending on the political outlook of any given community. You might see hardline Maoists in a town and, just a 15 minute drive later, encounter a fascist village that burns all men at stake. Sometimes, their leaders (singular or plural depending on the region) are chosen democratically, theocratically or even at random, like in the [Athenian democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athenian_democracy).
- The Carthians might very well be the only [Covenant](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Covenants "Category:Covenants") that acknowledges halflings like Dhampirs, Caitiffs and Ghouls as full-fledged members. Even if their ascendance to the highest positions is unlikely thanks to layers upon layers of glass ceilings, their social mobility is far wider within the movement than in the vampiric society at large.
- Their name originates from the [myth of Carthage in Vampire: The Masquerade](https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Carthage_\(WOD\)), supposedly a city where vampires and humans lived in harmony, with knowledge of one another.
