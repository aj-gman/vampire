==Ordinul, Dragonii, Ordinul Dragonului==
**Covenant of vampire scholars and thaumaturges using their quasi-Disciplines known as "Coils" in order to master the vampiric condition. Often isolated from the greater vampiric society, the Ordo Dracul claim to descend from the great Dracula, following in his footsteps to achieve the Great Work.**

#### Core
- **Advantage:** [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Alchimie\|Alchimie]], twisted versions of [[Core/Sisteme/VTR/Tainele Juramintelor/Magick\|Magick]] that bleed life into the vampiric condition.
- **Vibe:** Methodical, Hierarchical, Isolated, Intelligent, Cold, Planned
- **Stereotypes:** Drug Dealer, Method Actor, University Professor, Unassuming Student, Plastic Surgeon, Street Fighter
- **Album Theme:** _[New History Warfare Vol. 2: Judges](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kSJmwjVS22ip0TQsiLNQMkT949NYCo830&si=ZEdy6daEQy84a2Ut)_

#### Fun Facts

- Usually, Dragons speak in a highly technical jargon including made-up words only understood by other members of the Order. This has gotten so complex that navigating any Ordo Dracul institution requires knowledge of **an entire creole**, "Cadoul/The Gift" combining Latin, Romanian, English and German.
- The Dragons have a hierarchical structure similar to the Invictus, sporting titles and ranks according to their skill in their scholarly craft. However, the political machinations of the Invictus are rarely present amongst the Ordo, preferring an approach based on merit to a fault.
- Fractionality in the Ordo Dracul is rare, but three main branches of ideology can be seen based on the stated goal. We call them Factions[^4]. Those who want to perfect their bodies for personal gain or power **(Impaled)**, those who want to understand the Curse in order to better aid those around them **(Medicos)** and, lastly, those who want to achieve some sort of ascendence by invalidating the Beast and even by curing the Banes **(Maestros)**.
- There are also Camps[^5], which give way to a sort of religion amongst the Order.


# [[Romania/Bucuresti/Bucuresti\|Bucuresti]]

The Bucharest Lodge regards itself as the capital, due to the historical ties that bind. It is said that the Dragon himself resides in this chapter, although any Kindred who dares explore that ends up more Damned than before.

The immense power granted by this political-religious specter of the covenant progeny has <mark style="background: [[FF5582A6\|FF5582A6]];">cemented Bucharest as a city of the Ordo Dracul, with olden rites and clear hierarchies</mark>.
- *Assign each Player Character a hidden value from 1 to 10, 1 being the closest to the Grand Architect and 10 being the farthest away.* 

The Voivodes of Ordo Dracul do not care about appeasing the other covenants, but they find value in pitting them against one another and in giving them false seats at the decision-making table. The Order has survived in various configurations since the failure of the **Carthian Revolution of 1848**.

#### Principia Draconis
- No loyalty above one's study.
- Understand, accept and embrace purposeful change.
- Accountability for everything one is and does.

#### Flavor
- Ordo Dracul's rule is like if you let Chinese nuclear scientists in charge of birthrates
- Coils of Greed[^1], of Pride[^2] and of Wrath[^3] are the most common amongst the Dragons of Bucuresti, with the Grand Architect said to have mastered all Coils nearly perfectly;
- The most knowledgeable Dragons uphold the Maestro ideology, wanting to become real Renaissance men, wielding Blood Sorcery of all sorts, as well as personalized Coils;


#### Structure
- [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Ordo Dracul/Generalul\|Generalul]], known as Marele Voievod[^6].
	- Under him, the Sworn of Dracula fulfill the bureaucratic procedures.
	- The [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Ordo Dracul/Sworn of Mysteries\|Sworn of Mysteries]] deals with social structures, communicating and following the other Covenants, holding roundtables and organizing conferences. 
		- While this might seem to be his entire role, his more important work has to do with tracking, following and pinpointing each vampire's whereabouts, reminiscent of his past work with the [Securitate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Securitate).
	- The [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Ordo Dracul/Sworn of the Axe\|Sworn of the Axe]] is the Protector of the Lore, including rites and research. He is by far the most adept at combat. Although his violent role is mostly honorary (executions), when Covenants get out of line he steps in.
		- His pastime revolves around pushing around Carthians and the Invictus, who are the most prone to violence and territorial expansion.
	- The [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Ordo Dracul/Sworn of the Dying Light\|Sworn of the Dying Light]] is the scientist supreme, in charge of deciding the focus of the various schools of mysteries. Leading researcher and strategic policy planner.
		- A more important role than recognized, destroying or capturing other knowledge from the likes of Lancea or the Crones is a constant in his night-to-night.
		- He is the optimal communicator for in-between the Order. A small Dragon can call upon his master to call upon their master to contact the Sworn and obtain an audience.
