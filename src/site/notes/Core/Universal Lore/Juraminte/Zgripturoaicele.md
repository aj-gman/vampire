**RO: Ciorile, Gheonoaiele, Zripturoaicele**
**Covenant of outcasts and undesirables, banded together by the community they've formed around their forsaken status, Kindred or otherwise. Crones are universally hated and never understood. Somewhere, they're the anarchist rabble of the underclass that's being stepped on by the universal jackboot. Elsewhere, there's the peaceful community that has formed around the city's last park, protesting their right. Deep within the forests, they're the pagans dancing around a fire, singing cursed Irish ballads to summon the Dark Mother. They've all suffered at the hands of the majority, but they've got different responses to it: some want to be left alone to their own devices, some want revenge, some want a better world.**

## Between the Covenants

> Each of the following excerpts are part of a larger story and aim to provide subjective truths about the covenants. The first in line is always a perspective from within the covenant, followed by their closest allies and ending with their worst rivals.

### Crones

Love is passionate. Our coven is lovely. We dance the night away, it's beautiful. We revel in the darkness that so many try to take away from us. You know how much it hurts to never belong? Every time you fuckin' try to talk to them, every time you try to get better, you're just left outside in the rain. It's not even about the humility, I've learned to stop caring about that. No, it's about knowing that it'll always be like this. Well, fuck them. I have my own friends now and they'll never leave. They'd have nowhere to go, like me.

Even from when I was still a mortal child, I used to speak to my teddy bear. His name was Lucas, he told me when I was five. My parents didn't believe me. Instead of helping me, they cried like babies when I told them my period would come for the first time. Lucas helped me then. Lucas always knew what to do. He was an old spirit that was destined for me to have. Then, Lucas found me when I was 18. He gave me a vial, told me it was his last drop of menstrual blood before he got Embraced. I knew I was special then, when I drank the viscous liquid. It was tasty. He drove me out in the woods and we made love that night like wild animals. He asked to marry me. I said yes. The sweetest kiss I've ever felt. He drove me home that night. From 5AM to 10PM I slept so calmly. I woke up after dusk, from noises down in the dining room. When I got there, Lucas had just finished tied my mother to her chair. Her and dad were the tastiest flesh I've ever had. I love Lucas and Lucas loves me.

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Miscarea Cartiana\|Miscarea Cartiana]]

The Coven? Uneasy allies, at best. They know their shit around voodoo magic, and those wacko psychedelic trips when they talk to their Dark Mother are fun as hell to watch. I mean, who wouldn't love to get killed over and over again by naked women? Fucked to submission and back, and they all look the same? That's a fun orgy I've ever heard of one. But they don't know when to stop. They have no control, they don't want anything than to waste the night partying in their occultist rituals. They're the first to say no to outside help. The other day, one of us, a licensed psychotherapist, went out into the woods. He came back this morning, almost burnt to a crisp, spouting nonsense. That costs resources, y'know? To bring him back from the brink of insanity. Resources we don't have, because we're waging war on all fronts with those nosey Estate bastards that think they're above everyone.
