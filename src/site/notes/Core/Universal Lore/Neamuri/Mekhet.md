## _The Ones You Can't See..._
*The smile of the all-knowing. The paranoia of being seen naked in the shower by a stranger carefully watching. A detective in a film noir going past his dues. "The world we don't see" - and the only reason the Masquerade is still alive. How can you believe your next of kin when they claim they saw a monster just moments ago, smiling in the corner?*

*Quiet. Dark, distressful and shadowy quiet. But it is not fear or horror that comes out of that quiet, no, it is paranoia. It's that moment where you hallucinate something that isn't there - or was it just a moment ago? They could be right behind that door, or maybe right behind you, watching, creeping, observing.*

*If the tables were reversed, maybe you'd love this power fantasy. They prey on your lack of ignorance. You are aware they exist, perhaps, or you hope that what you saw in that foggy mirror was actually there, but you never can truly know.*

*Some people fear the shadows, but we are the Shadows. Go anywhere, see anything, know it all - an intricate web woven of intrigue and despair. Touch it, change it, whisper the truth in one's ear and lies in another's. Play God with them.*

*Do you know how much it hurts to look in the mirror and not see anything? Do you know how much it hurts to be burdened with so much knowledge and suffering and just horrible, horrible things happening to nice people and beautiful things happening to those who don't deserve it. And I cannot intervene to do a thing.*

**But it's alright in it's own way. I like to watch. So pretend I am simply not there.**

### Clan Bane / Blestemul de Neam
**The Tenebrous Curse**: Mekhet Kindred are more easily repelled by fire and sunlight, finding it harder to resist the urge to flee. This is because theirs is the blood damned to relive time and time again moments they cannot share with another. All Banes caused by Detachment are treated more harshly.

### The Beast / Fiara
The Mekhet Beast is strangely silent compared to the Beasts of other Clans, making it hard for the Mekhet to distinguish what actions were his own and what actions were the result of the Beast's influence. Their kiss feels like revelation, like a corpse-cold epiphany, and their victims experience it like a surge of enlightenment, like they are on the verge of realizing some great secret or insight. For Mekhet, their Vessels are puzzles, means to find an enlightenment hidden in each drop of stolen blood.

## Within the Covenants

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Miscarea Cartiana\|Miscarea Cartiana]]
The Movement is all about the signal. Often, visibility is preferred to stealth. But when the fighting gets dirty, the Revolution needs Shadows. Reporters and spies, the Mekhet are the ones to unlock all of the establishment’s hidden closets and become intimate with every skeleton within. They get the juicy details to broadcast over the Cacophony. They are the whistleblowers piping at decibels only their fellow Beasts can hear. Who better to go muckraking in this weird world?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Zgripturoaicele\|Zgripturoaicele]]
The Mekhet cannot help but seek out occulted secrets. Some are willing to dive into the pitch dark of the Crone’s maw in order to seize those secrets. The Mother’s Army, in turn, finds use for the Shadows. Their inquisitive minds take to blood sorcery with a frightening alacrity. The feverish beat of the Crone’s drums can quicken even the icy passions of a Shadow. Some Mekhet can even leave their bodies and take to the spiritual plane during rituals. Isn’t magic more fun when you’re dead?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Invictus\|Invictus]]
Who better to enforce the Conspiracy of Silence? Mekhet make fine spymasters for the First Estate. The only downside is that they are too good at this function, making covenant members of other clans nervous. Shadows often serve as agents and advisors, but some rise quite high. How terrifying is the prince who might know your every transgression at a glance? A Shadow prince might walk amongst his Kindred, wearing their faces. When a Firebrand baits you by asking your opinion of your liege…how can you be sure?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Lancea et Sanctum\|Lancea et Sanctum]]
Some Mekhet come for the magic, and find religious zeal afterwards. The Sanctified are happy to induct a Shadow, as she can touch a relic and fathom its deepest secrets. Who knows what sins lurk in the minds of mortals? Who better to give the Lord’s Judgment? Who else has the all-seeing eyes of God?

### [[Core/Universal Lore/Juraminte/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]]
Simply put, the Order needs the Mekhet. It is the Shadows who can best sniff out dragon nests, and it is the Shadows most likely to determine the nature and use of those nests. Their supernatural senses are invaluable in the Order’s experiments. The Mekhet can more directly see what other members of the covenant grope for in the dark with countless rounds of trial and error (mostly error). With the Shadows’ natural obsession for riddle-solving, symbol-deciphering, and delving into mysteries best left alone, the Order hardly needs to give them orders. It merely has to let the Mekhet be.
