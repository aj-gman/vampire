### Corebook
##### Mechanics
p. 84-87 Mask & Dirge
this is a mixed bag for me - sometimes the mask & dirge actually inform my character decisions, but sometimes they feel pointless when my character breaks out of their mold; since they are the only way to recover Willpower, it's a good way to encourage the Player to stick to their beliefs and their personality

p. 301 Conditions
even more of a mixed bag because they're cool, but you need a fucking book to keep track of them and know which one to give out.[^2]

p. 169-170 - rolling dice
you need only 1 success (but success is only 8, 9, 10);[^1]

p. 170-171 - extended actions, really good for research and things that happen over the course of time

p 172-173 - Interesting actions (research, time, social maneuvering)
- social maneuvering e cel cu Doors system, which I find pretty good for extended actions to "cultivate" your prey, befriend them etc., because most people freeze irl when you try to press them into doing something against their nature

p. 175-178 - Combat
- Guns are deadly, even for vampires - but you've got ways to prevent getting shot ([[Core/Systems/VTR/Disciplines/Fortitude\|Fortitude]], [[Core/Systems/VTR/Disciplines/Potence\|Potence]], [[Core/Systems/VTR/Disciplines/Celerity\|Celerity]])
- Defense as a STAT (lower Attacker's dice by your Defense, lose 1 Defense until end of round)

p. 142-149 (+ mai exista o carte dedicata)
- Oh, you want cool Disciplines or perhaps just a cool ABILITY for your character that they learn because of their Clan/Covenant etc.? => Devotions
- Like how V5 tried to do Amalgams, but it's hyperspecific and can be made up on the spot

##### Lore
p. 278-280 Extra juicy
- have other players rp each other's beast
- personify disciplines
- how to portray touchstones and the blackbox scene

p. 288-296 ghouls and mortals and how to place them in the world

p. 14-28 the clans
really good descriptions similar to V20 on how the clans think of themselves and of one another, as well as what they can do in each covenant, really good for character creation and inspiration;

the clans are great because they symbolize vampirism as a `parasite of humanity`: all of these clans relate to a certain fear or excess that humans have
each clan also has `a specific beast behavior`, `a clan bane that triggers often`, `a specific associated discipline`

`daeva` are ministry, toreador, malkavian - they obsess over their beauty, over their prey, they can be entrancing and rule the world but they can also fall hopelessly in love with a mortal they fed on twice;
	only they have `majesty` (presence)

`gangrel` are gangrel, brujah, nosferatu - they are the ones most capable of controlling their beast, they aren't animal handlers as much as they are violent weapons capable of inflicting so much pain but also can "behave" themselves if given reason to
	only they have `protean`

`mekhet` are banu haqim, nosferatu, lasombra - they are the ones most tormented within themselves, as they are shadows that can't come into the light (even faint lights), they know every single secret but they are always forced to be spymasters, not take the spotlight
	only they have `auspex`

`nosferatu` are nosferatu, malkavian, hecata - they are just as obsessed as the `daeva`, but they love to inflict pain (instinctually) and their clan bane is some sort of defect, malformation, not necessarily physical[^3]; they have a scary outside and they aren't forced into the shadows; they are nightmares, not shadows. 
	only they have `nightmare`, which is dementation's horror cousin

`ventrue` are tzimitzi, ventrue, tremere and lasombra - they have animalism, interestingly, which is explained by them being able to rule over their territory, feel every single step in their domain (like a tzimitzi); they're the most similar to what you know
	only they have `dominate`

- Disciplines are very similar to V20
- Covenants: the rules for the quasi-disciplines that each covenant gets access to are super interesting and very very very good: https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Quasi


[^1]: this isnt in the rules, but i like still maintaining a level of difficulty by saying/thinking "even if you need 1 success, you need 3 successes to complete this action without consequences" whenever I believe that the situation warrants extra risk.

[^2]: Conditions, i'd say, are useful when they are vague: "High". This is the case with Neon City Overdrive, where you can add dice or substract dice by saying "this would trigger my condition". Games where players decide what the condition means (and get vetoed by the GM) make Conditions actually feel impactful
