**HAUNTING THE NARATIVE. 

Childer of [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Cartieni/Cacanache\|Cacanache]], brother of [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Cartieni/Fane\|Fane]].  He had spend his mortal years in [[Romania/Bucuresti/București\|București]], as the older brother in a family of four. At 18, he joined the army, as he had no real inclinations, but a strong desire to escape his hometown. The birth of his little sister brought him back, and his service reshaped his set of priorities. The constant come and go, the rarer nights spend home, and the intense special missions and interventions drove him to easy escapes that numb the pain. He, of course, got kicked out. Both from home and the army. The only truth that he tells his sister, to this day.

Matei became the perfect target one winter night in 2012, near the Carpati train station. Cold, drugged, and regretful, was approached by [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Cartieni/Cacanache\|Cacanache]]. A deal was made.

After [[Core/Lore Universal/Îmbrățișarea\|Îmbrățișarea]], Carpati was trained to become the eyes and years of [[Romania/Bucuresti/București\|the city]]. He got sent to [[Rome\|Rome]], just to study [[The Carthian Law\|The Carthian Law]]. Make that change, create connections, bring the revolution to life. Join [[Core/Lore Universal/Jurăminte/Mișcarea Cartiană\|the movement]], and then bring it home. Another job well done. The pride and joy of [[Core/Lore Universal/Neamuri/Bloodlines/Azerkatil\|the Azerkatils]].

Every now and then, he used to visit his sister, now about of primary school age. Feed her white lies, for the sake of protecting her. He used to own a rooftop bar with his [[Romania/Bucuresti/București\|București]] coterie. The death of [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Cartieni/Cacanache\|his sire]] wrapped Carpati in great confusion and made him hallucinate.

Last spotted: [[Romania/Bucuresti/Bucuresti Map\|Parcul Tineretului (or maybe it was Parcul Carol)]]. Disappeared, (un)officially dead in [[Core/Lore Universal/Jurăminte/Ordo Dracul\|Ordo Dracul]] records.
