Does he, perhaps, suffer from [[Malkavia\|Malkavia]]?
Sired in 1053 de catre un Print caruia i se prevestise moartea. El era un simplu bufon, devenit acum sol pentru aceasta veste. Sburatorul isi doreste sa ajunga in Paradis, dar este Grounded de acest loc, blestemat sa il cutreiere dandu-le vesti si muribunzilor, si imbratisatilor.  

Rece si Hedonist.

He can only feed from virgins. He is a Bondslave to a [[Core/Universal Lore/Neamuri/Bloodlines/Salubri\|Salubri]]. High-functioning addict. Drug of choice is snow. Although his Humanity isn't exactly high (or low), he uses his senses in order to double the effects of Blush of Life. Zeroed - he simply does not exist, his face unrecognizable.
