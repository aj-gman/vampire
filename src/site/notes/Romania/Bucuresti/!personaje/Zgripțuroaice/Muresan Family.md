A family of free [[Core/Lore Universal/Specii/Gargui\|Gargui]] protected by [[Core/Lore Universal/Jurăminte/Zgripțuroaicele\|the Circle's]] [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Zgripțuroaice/Nina\|Nina]], who they refer to as "Mama-Soacra", despite acting as if she is a mother of the group. They have been all created by [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Ordo Dracul/Răducanu\|Răducanu]], which is **his greatest shame** - because they escaped him and they didn't love him "properly". They were supposed to protect his haven, but eventually escaped after a [[Core/Lore Universal/Jurăminte/Mișcarea Cartiană\|Carthian]] discovered the way he was abusing them. Still, all members of the [[Romania/Bucuresti/!personaje/Zgripțuroaice/Muresan Family\|Muresan Family]] 
- Father, Razvan Muresan. Twisted face, visibly broken stone. He flies heavy and always mumbles.
