## [Principles](https://www.reddit.com/r/DMAcademy/comments/6u5403/guide_how_to_run_a_successful_online_west/)
- _**Make sure the all the DMs are willing to work and develop the world together.**_
- _**Decide on a good premise and spend time on meaningful world-building**_
	- _Make sure that your individual sessions are actually good_
	- _Make sure your premise and your sessions account for all types of players_
	- _Make good story arcs and global events to keep things fresh_
	- _Make sure to have the players be equal_
	- _Decide on a context of the players banding together_
- _**Be open to player interaction with the concept of your campaign, and give them the tools to give them agency**_
- _**Keep track of all the moving parts**_
- _**Keep track of the state of the world**_
- _**Set up the policy on the magical items, trading, crafting, downtime and multiple PCs per player**_
