#npc #moldova #vampire 

A Toreador antitribu by VTM standards, [[Romania/Moldova/Characters/Bloody Mary\|Bloody Mary]] is a young vampire who tried to rise above the ranks and overcome [[Romania/Moldova/Characters/Lidia\|Lidia]]'s brewing revolt against [[Romania/Moldova/Characters/Isac Maxim\|the Cardinal]]. Unfortunately, being part of [[Romania/Moldova/Characters/Olimpia Moldovan\|Olimpia Moldovan]]'s crew meant she was the first to find out about this attempt to ruin [[Romania/Moldova/Characters/Isac Maxim\|the Cardinal]], so she was almost - almost killed. A Ravnos showed her a trick, how to hide in the mirrors and reflections of windows. After having learnt that, she hid in [[Romania/Moldova/Locations/Tudor Plaza\|Tudor Plaza]] until the events of [[Romania/Moldova/Stories/Killing Time\|Killing Time]], where she got too greedy for her own good... again.

[[Core/Sisteme/HTV/Nest\|Nest]]: [[Romania/Moldova/Locations/The Hall of Mirrors\|The Hall of Mirrors]] in [[Romania/Moldova/Locations/Tudor Plaza\|Tudor Plaza]]

### Characteristics
- **Virtue:** Patient
- **Vice:** Wrathful
- **Archetype:** Rockstar (Innovative, Passion) | Bold, Artistic, Unorthodox
- **Talent:** Once per scene, when someone fulfills a tenet of Bloody Mary’s “rules,” the player can roll her Stamina + Occult. Successes on this roll may be applied on one of the following: restoring spent Willpower, healing damage (one success for a bashing wound, two for lethal, three for aggravated), or applying the bonuses to rolls in pursuit of their goal. After this has been activated, successes that haven’t been spent are discarded.
- **Frailty:** Narrative Chains — Bloody Mary was valued for her appearance when she was alive; now, she must remain in sight of a mirror. When not reflected in a mirror, she can’t use her Dread Powers, including vacating her victim’s body. The full-length mirrors in her nest (p. XX) sustain her, causing her visible discomfort whenever one of them is broken; and breaking them all before Bloody Mary vacates her final victim will free her victims and destroy her forever. Characters investigating Bloody Mary’s Tell gain an exceptional success on three successes rather than five.

### Powers
**Special:** Suffers from the Addicted (Murder) Condition. Bloody Mary may use the following **Dread Powers: Black Gate, Body Thief, Eye Spy.** 

**Tell:** Bloody Mary can only be summoned by teenage girls performing the ritual of saying her name into a mirror in a dark room 13 times. She brings each victim to Tudor Plaza one by one, poses them, and vacates their body, which transforms into a mannequin. At least a day after that, she visits another of her summoners and does the same. Once all are dead, she goes into hibernation until the next time a group of girls summons her. A woman or teenage girl who didn’t summon Bloody Mary can invite her to possess them instead, which spares the lives of the current crop of victims (reversing their transformation into mannequins), but it frees the Slasher to live out the volunteer’s life, which she uses to commit more murders without the need for a summons.

- **Black Gate:** Bloody Mary can use mirrored surfaces to instantaneously travel up to 60 yards. To open the gate, she spends 3 Willpower. The portal remains open for 3 minutes and can be used only by Bloody Mary and any victims she drags through it. One end of the portal must lead to her nest. 
- **Body Thief:** Once per day, Bloody Mary may enact a ritual lasting an hour and spend 1 Willpower to vacate her current victim’s body, which transforms into a mannequin. She recovers spent Willpower at the rate of one per day. Once she has recovered all spent Willpower, Bloody Mary possesses the next of her summoners to see her own reflection.
- **Eye Spy:** Bloody Mary can use mirrors across the mall to spy on visitors, her face visible in the reflective surface. She remains aware of her surroundings while surveilling. Using this costs 1 Willpower per hour of use.
